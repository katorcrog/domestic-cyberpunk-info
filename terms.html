Who it is for

Domestic Cyberpunk Sleep is maintained for its owner's personal household use. These public pages provide information about the project. They do not provide public registration, a paid service or access to the private notebook.

Authorized use

Use of the private notebook is limited to the owner and household members or administrators authorized by the owner. Google Health access requires the account holder's consent. Keep private sign-in details and connection credentials private.

Records and annotations

Google Health and device records may have gaps, revisions or overlapping versions. Day notes reflect the person who entered them. The project is being built to preserve the distinction between imported measurements and household annotations.

Availability and changes

This personal project is still being developed. Features, availability and the Google connection may change. Access can also end if Google permission is withdrawn or expires.

Privacy and ending access

The privacy information describes the requested data, storage, planned chat integration and how to revoke Google's permission or arrange removal of local copies.
